# References

This directory contains the references associated with the design and simulation of conventional 6T SRAM and radiation-hardened DICE SRAM, with emphasis on Single Event Upsets (SEUs), radiation-hardening techniques, and memory reliability.

## Research Papers

1. W. Zhang, P. Li, P. Guo, and Y. Chen, “Multi-Level Single Event Effect-Hardened Design for SRAM-FPGA-Based System,” in *Proceedings of the 14th International Conference on Reliability, Maintainability and Safety (ICRMS)*, Urumqi, China, 2023, pp. 1452–1455. doi: [10.1109/ICRMS59672.2023.00247](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRMS59672.2023.00247).

2. J.-Y. Oh and S.-H. Jo, “Radiation-Hardened 16T SRAM Cell with Improved Read and Write Stability for Space Applications,” *Applied Sciences*, vol. 14, no. 11, Art. no. 11940, 2024.

3. Z. Ming et al., “Research on Single Event Upset Cross-Section of DICE SRAM,” in *Proceedings of the Prognostics and System Health Management Conference (PHM-Harbin)*, Harbin, China, 2017.

4. M. S. Gorbunov, A. B. Boruzdina, and P. S. Dolotov, “Semi-Empirical Method for Estimation of Single-Event Upset Cross-Section for SRAM DICE Cells,” in *Proceedings of the 15th European Conference on Radiation and Its Effects on Components and Systems (RADECS)*, Moscow, Russia, 2015.

5. Y. Xiong, N. J. Pieper, J. B. Kronenberg, D. R. Ball, N. A. Dodds, and B. L. Bhuva, “Comparative Study of Radiation-Hardened-by-Design (RHBD) Techniques at a 3-nm Bulk FinFET Node,” *IEEE Transactions on Nuclear Science*, vol. 72, no. 8, pp. 2793–2798, Aug. 2025.

---

These references cover SRAM radiation hardening, DICE-cell reliability, SEU characterization, and radiation-hardened-by-design techniques.
